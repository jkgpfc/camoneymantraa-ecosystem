Refund and Cancellation Policy

Tape Reader Bot · CA Money Mantra · Last updated 25 November 2026

Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends at the end of the paid period unless you pay again.

When we refund

  • Duplicate or wrong charge: if you were charged twice for the same period, or charged an amount different from the plan shown, we refund the extra amount in full.
  • Paid but no access: if your payment succeeded but access was not switched on and we cannot fix it within 2 working days of your email, we refund the payment in full.
  • Change of mind within 7 days: you may ask for a full refund within 7 days of your first-ever payment.
  • Long outage: if the service is unavailable for more than 7 days in a row during a paid period, we refund the unused part of that period on request.

Other than these cases, payments for a period that has started are not refundable.

How to ask

Email camoneymantraa@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_ and is shown on screen after payment and in Razorpay's receipt email) and the email you signed in with.

Timing

Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more working days to show the credit.

Privacy Policy

Tape Reader Bot · CA Money Mantra · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what Tape Reader Bot (operated under the name CA Money Mantra) collects and why.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and the time you sign in. We do not get your Google password.
  • Payments: for each payment we keep the plan, amount, Razorpay order and payment IDs, the email the payment is linked to, and the payment time. Razorpay processes the payment itself; we never receive your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to the data you give them.
  • On your device: your settings, filters and downloaded market data are stored in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device.

Why

  • To let you sign in, link your paid access to your account and show how long it lasts.
  • To record who accessed the tool and to resolve payment questions.

Where it is kept

Subscription and payment records are stored on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers KV). The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Payment records are kept for about 400 days, and access records until your access ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data and do not use it for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Google, Razorpay, Cloudflare, GitHub) to run the service, or where the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

You can use the service as a guest without signing in. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by writing to camoneymantraa@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (for example, payment records) are kept for the period required.

Children

The service is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
